# ValioKey — Beta Support

ValioKey is a zero-knowledge password manager for iPhone with system AutoFill. This small TestFlight beta is free and requires iOS 27 or later. Family sharing and the browser extension are not included.

**Use made-up test logins only.** Do not store real passwords in this beta.

## Report a problem

In TestFlight, open **ValioKey** and choose **Send Beta Feedback**. Include the app version and build, iPhone model, iOS version, what you did, what you expected and what happened. Never capture or send a master password, a recovery key or real login details.

Public support email: [valiokey@valioguard.com](mailto:valiokey@valioguard.com)

## Keep your recovery key

Your recovery key is shown when you set up your account. Write it down and keep it somewhere other than your iPhone. ValioKey cannot read your master password or recovery key. **If you lose both, your vault cannot be recovered, and we cannot do it for you.**

## Forgot your master password

On the sign-in screen, choose **Forgot your master password?** and enter your email and recovery key. We email you a code, and you choose a new master password. Your other devices are then told to sign in again.

## A new iPhone

Install ValioKey from TestFlight and sign in. We email a code to confirm the new device.

## Turn on AutoFill

Open iPhone **Settings → General → AutoFill & Passwords**, turn on **ValioKey**, then open a sign-in page in Safari.

## Delete your account

In the app, open **Settings → Account → Delete account**. Deletion takes effect after a 7-day grace period, during which you can cancel. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md) for what remains and for how long.

## Known beta limits

- Beta data may be reset. We will give notice by email except in an emergency.
- The app needs a network connection to create an account and to sync.
- After a recovery, a device that still has the old copy of your account can open that local copy with the old master password, but it cannot sync until you sign in again.
- TestFlight builds expire after 90 days. Install the update when a new beta is provided.
